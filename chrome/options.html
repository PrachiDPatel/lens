Privacy

Your key lives in this browser profile's local storage. It is not encrypted at rest — anyone with access to the profile could read it, so prefer a key you can rotate. Requests go directly from your browser to api.anthropic.com; there is no middleman. The key is only ever sent to Anthropic, and only when you click Send to Claude.
